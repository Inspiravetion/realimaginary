## Glossary: 
*** NOTE: these may be out of date ***
- `Resource`: An article, pdf, podcast, video, audio, or any other digital media. `Resources` have associated `Content Tags`.
- `Module`: A guided collection of `Resources` and `Activities` that covers a discrete concept. `Modules` inherit all of the `Content Tags` of their `Resources`
- `Course`: A guided collection of `Modules`, `Resources`, and `Activities` that covers a broad concept. `Courses` inherit all of the `Content Tags` of their `Modules` and `Resources`
- `Content Tag`: Curated topical tags that cover the spectrum of social justice concepts ex. "Prison Industrial Complex", "Environmental Justice", or "LGBTQIA+ Rights"
